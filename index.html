RKSK DIAGNOSIS NON-DIAGNOSTIC SCREENING PHILOSOPHY

Ethical Visual Estimation

RKSK Diagnosis is developed under a strict ethical framework. The system provides visual wellness observations based on surface optics, illumination consistency, and facial landmark stability—never claiming diagnostic certainty.

Multi-Spectral Neural Modeling

Our computer-vision engine isolates 68 facial fiducial coordinates to gauge subtle micro-vascular chromatic shifts and symmetry profiles, computing non-invasive baseline indices.

Clinical Responsibility Notice Facial screening cannot replace diagnostic blood tests, biopsies, or licensed clinical assessments. Always seek professional physician guidance for any health symptoms.
RKSK SYSTEM PIPELINE HOW THE SCANNER OPERATES

1. Optic Frame Acquisition

High-fidelity frame capture occurs directly inside your client browser. No biometric face imagery is uploaded or saved to remote databases, maintaining total confidentiality.

2. Facial Mesh Localization

Real-time geometry checks segment key facial domains: the frontal region, ocular aperture, periorbital zone, and mandibular contour.

3. Chromatic & Symmetry Extraction

Light variance and surface texture parameters are normalized against ambient tone references to detect visible fatigue and hydration indicators.

4. Indicator Synthesis

Visual observations are classified into five wellness signals accompanied by calibrated confidence ratings.
